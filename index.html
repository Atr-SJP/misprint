How to play

  1. 01Roll

    Steer the roller with the mouse, ← → or A D. Click or Space serves the ball — it heads the way you’re moving.

  2. 02Smash

    Click again right as the ball meets the roller. A smashed ball punches through layers, and through black blocks.

  3. 03Bleed

    The ball soaks up the colour it peels. Hit that colour again and the ink bleeds: the whole connected patch peels at once.

  4. 04Clear the plate

    Peel every coloured layer, then pick a technique. Rarer ones turn up as the run goes on, and many pair up with each other. Three bosses stand between you and the finished edition.
