# MISPRINT

Breakout, printed in three inks.

**▶ [Play it in your browser](https://atr-sjp.github.io/misprint/)**

Peel coloured ink off printed plates. The ball soaks up the colour it peels — hit that colour again and the whole connected patch bleeds away at once. Time a click as the ball lands to Smash straight through layers and black blocks.

Nine plates, three bosses, and forty techniques to collect along the way — from Open Edition commons to the rare Artist's Proof — many of which pair up. Every plate you clear becomes a print you keep.

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Move | Mouse, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> — on a phone, drag |
| Serve / Smash | Click or <kbd>Space</kbd> as the ball meets the roller — on a phone, tap with a second finger |
| Pause | <kbd>P</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> |
| Mute | <kbd>M</kbd> |

## Running it yourself

No build step and no dependencies: open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server.
